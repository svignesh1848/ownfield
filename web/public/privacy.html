Privacy notice

This notice explains how this OwnField installation handles personal data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP).

What we collect

Names, work email, phone numbers, role, sign-in activity (time, IP address, device type), and the business records you enter (outlets, orders, invoices, visits with location, photos, expenses, leave).

Why

Only to run the sales and distribution service for your company: signing in, keeping accounts secure, recording field work, and billing. We do not sell data or use it for advertising.

Your consent and rights

Company sign-up records consent to this notice. You can ask your company administrator to see, correct, export (JSON/CSV) or delete your data. Deleted users are anonymised; tax records the law requires are kept without personal identifiers where possible.

Security and retention

Passwords are hashed, sign-ins are rate-limited with lockout, optional two-step sign-in is available, and admin actions are logged. Database backups are kept for 7 days.

Contact

For any privacy request or grievance, contact your company administrator, who is the data fiduciary for your company's data. The operator of this installation can be reached through that administrator.
