Security and your data

Your sales data is your business. Here is how OwnField keeps it yours.

Who can see what

Every user has a role: admin, manager, rep, distributor or retailer. Each role only sees the screens and data it needs. Reps see their own beats and outlets, distributors see their own stock and orders, and retailers see their own shop.

Sign-in

Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text. Admins can turn on a 6-digit authenticator code for extra protection, and can reset a user's access.

Take your data out anytime

Export to Excel, use the API and webhooks, query your data in the SQL data workspace, or connect BI tools. You are never locked in.

Self-host if you want

OwnField is open source under the GNU AGPL v3. You can run it on your own server so the data never leaves your control. Ask us for access to the source code.

Location and camera

Location and camera are used only for features your company turns on, such as GPS check-in and selfie attendance.

Hosted service

For questions about where hosted data is stored, backups and our security practices, email hello@ownfield.in. We do not hold SOC 2 or ISO certifications today, and we will not claim them until we do. See also our Privacy Policy.
