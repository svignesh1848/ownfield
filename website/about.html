About OwnField

OwnField is an open-source field sales and distributor management app, built in India for Indian businesses.

Why we built it

OwnField was started by Vignesh S, who works in supply chain management. Too many brands and distributors still run field sales on WhatsApp, phone calls and Excel, while the big tools need long contracts and keep your data in their system. OwnField is the alternative: free to start, open source, and yours to keep.

What we believe

  • You should be able to try software before you talk to sales.
  • Your data belongs to you, and you can take it out anytime.
  • Software should work in your team's language and on any phone.
  • No fake numbers. We will share results only when real customers have measured them.

Open source

OwnField is licensed under the GNU AGPL v3. Ask us for access to the source code.
