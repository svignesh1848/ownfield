The problem

Your sales team is out there. Can you see what is happening?

Most brands and distributors still run field sales on WhatsApp, phone calls and Excel. That works until it doesn't.

  1. No visibilityYou don't know which shops your reps visited today, or what they sold, until the evening call.
  2. Orders get lostOrders come in by WhatsApp and phone, get typed again, and mistakes reach the invoice.
  3. Distributor stock is a guessYou find out a distributor ran out after the retailer has already bought from someone else.
  4. Billing and GST take too longInvoices, van sales and returns are done by hand or in a separate tool that doesn't talk to the field.
  5. Reports come lateBy the time the monthly Excel is ready, the month is over and it is too late to act.
  6. Software is costly and lockedBig SFA tools need long contracts and sales calls, and your data sits in their system.

How it works

Live in days, not months

No app store, no long rollout. Four steps from sign-up to the first order.

  1. Set upAdd products, outlets, distributors, users and GST details. Import them from Excel.
  2. Plan beatsAssign routes and targets, and turn on geo-fencing or selfie attendance if you want.
  3. Sell in the fieldReps open the app on any phone, check in at outlets and take orders, even on weak networks.

FAQ

Questions

How do I start?

Fill in the Start free form. We'll get in touch to set up your account, or show you a walkthrough first if you prefer.

Do reps need to install an app from the Play Store?

No. The rep app is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Reps open a link and add it to the home screen. It works on weak networks and syncs when the network returns.

Does it support GST invoicing?

Yes. GST invoices and credit notes, with e-invoice and e-way bill settings.

Which languages are supported?

English plus the 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule, each in its own script. A few translations are still being completed and fall back to English for now.

Can it connect to our ERP or other tools?

Yes. It has a generic API, webhooks and file exports, and you can connect BI tools such as Power BI and Tableau.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export everything at any time, or self-host so the data stays on your own server.
