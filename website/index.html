FAQ

Is OwnField really free?

Yes. The open-source edition is free to self-host under the AGPL v3. Paid plans are for hosting, support and onboarding, so contact us for a quote.

Do reps need to install an app from the Play Store?

No. The rep app is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Reps open it in the browser and can add it to the home screen. It also works offline and syncs when the network returns.

Does it support GST invoicing?

Yes. OwnField creates Indian GST invoices and includes e-invoice and e-way bill settings.

Which languages are supported?

English plus 20+ Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese and Urdu.

Can it connect to our ERP or planning tools?

Yes. It has a generic API, webhooks and file exports for connecting to systems such as SAP, o9 and Kinaxis. You can also connect BI tools such as Power BI and Tableau.

Is it only for FMCG?

No. OwnField works for any industry. It suits any business that sells through field reps, distributors and retailers.

Who owns the data?

You do. When you self-host, the data stays on your own server. On hosted plans you can export everything at any time.
