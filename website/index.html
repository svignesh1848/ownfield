FAQ

Can I try it without talking to sales?

Yes. Open the live demo with sample data, or start free with your own company.

Do reps need to install an app from the Play Store?

No. The rep app is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Reps open a link in the browser and add it to the home screen. It works on weak networks and syncs when the network returns.

Does it support GST invoicing?

Yes. OwnField creates Indian GST invoices and includes e-invoice and e-way bill settings.

Which languages are supported?

English plus Indian languages, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese and Urdu.

Can it connect to our ERP or other tools?

Yes. It has a generic API, webhooks and file exports, and you can connect BI tools such as Power BI and Tableau.

Is it only for FMCG?

No. It suits any business that sells through field reps, distributors and retailers.

Who owns the data?

You do. You can export everything at any time, or self-host so the data stays on your own server.
