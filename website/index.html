Questions

Can I try it without talking to sales?

Yes. Open the live demo with sample data, or start free with your own company.

Do reps need to install an app from the Play Store?

No. The rep app is a Progressive Web App (PWA). Reps open a link and add it to the home screen. It works on weak networks and syncs when the network returns.

Does it support GST invoicing?

Yes. GST invoices and credit notes, with e-invoice and e-way bill settings.

Which languages are supported?

English plus the 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule, each in its own script. A few translations are still being completed and fall back to English for now.

Can it connect to our ERP or other tools?

Yes. It has a generic API, webhooks and file exports, and you can connect BI tools such as Power BI and Tableau.

Is it only for FMCG?

No. It suits FMCG, apparel, pharma, building materials, agri inputs and any business that sells through reps, distributors and retailers.

Who owns the data?

You do. Export everything at any time, or self-host so the data stays on your own server.
