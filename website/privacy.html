Privacy Policy

This is a short, plain-language summary. Last updated: 2 November 2026.

This website

The ownfield.in website does not use tracking cookies, analytics or advertising. The contact form opens your own email app, so nothing you type is stored by this website. Our web server keeps standard access logs, such as IP address and pages visited, for security and troubleshooting.

The OwnField app

If your company uses the hosted OwnField app at app.ownfield.in, your company controls the business data it enters, such as users, outlets, orders, invoices, attendance and location data. We process that data only to provide the service. We do not sell it. Location and camera access are used only for features your company turns on, such as GPS check-in and selfie attendance.

Self-hosted installations

If you run OwnField on your own servers, we do not receive your data.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you. Where your company is the data controller, we will help it respond to your request.
